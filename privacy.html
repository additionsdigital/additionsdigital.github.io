Additions

Privacy Policy. Effective October 13, 2026.

Additions Digital LLC ("Additions") runs this website and a private assistant application that connects to Google accounts. This policy explains what information each one handles and how.

This website

This site has no forms, accounts, analytics or advertising, and it does not set cookies. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the site is delivered through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare. Those services receive standard request information, such as your IP address and browser type, under their own privacy policies.

The assistant application

The assistant is a private tool used only by the owner of Additions with his own Google account. It is not offered to the public. When that account owner signs in with Google and grants access, the assistant can use:

  • Gmail, to read and organize email for that account owner.
  • Google Calendar, to read the account owner's calendars and create or update events on them.

How Google user data is used

  • Data is used only to provide the assistant's features to the account owner who granted access.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles of anyone.
  • To complete a request, relevant content may be sent to an AI model provider for processing. It is not shared with anyone else, except where required by law.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored on a private computer controlled by the account owner and are not shared.

Additions' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page. Once access is revoked, the assistant can no longer read or change Gmail or Calendar data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
